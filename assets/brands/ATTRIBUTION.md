## Mercado Libre

Simple Icons has no Mercado Libre mark (only Mercado Pago, a different product).
Mercado Libre’s public Legal Brand Book restricts unauthorized logo use and does
not publish a general-purpose press-kit SVG for third-party apps.

BERA therefore identifies Mercado Libre with **text only**. No handshake/logo
file is bundled, and no unofficial third-party SVG is used.
